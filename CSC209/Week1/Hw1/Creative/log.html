title: The Phantom of the Opera

type: Musical, Film

rating: 4/5

comment(s):

I recently watched Phantom of the Opera (2004), which led me to listen to different recordings of the musical on Spotify. I love the original London cast recording (1987) and the Royal Albert Hall recording (2011)! I like the movie too, but there is something about Christine in the London and Royal Albert Hall recording that I love.

As for the title song, 'Phantom of the Opera', I also love the performance of Lucy St. Louis's Christine! Her performance was the one that made me look into the musical. From the different Phantoms, my favorites are those who lower their voice when commanding Christine to sing for him. Such as Antonio Banderas' performance as the Phantom. It just makes it so much more sensual. In the stage plays I've seen, I go crazy when I see the Phantom wave their hands to command Christine to face in different directions as she continues to sing for him. It makes the Phantom all that more entrancing.

Other songs I loved during my first listen was 'Angel of Music' and 'Music of the Night'. As for the other songs, I think I need to give them more of a listen and see their different stage plays. I'm so sad that I won't get the chance to see it live. I would have loved to see the stage play for the first time in person.

In the first few numbers, I felt so much chemistry between Erik and Christine. I found myself hoping she would give into the "darkness". However, she chose Raoul. He is the acceptable and safe choice. He isn't complicated or unfamiliar. They are cute and I do love their musical number together, but as a couple, they didn't really stand out to me. So, I kind of hated that she chose the safe, boring option.
